**Trussen AI** is the AI that works inside your workspace. Available on **Premium**.

It's different from **AI Assistance**, the Help bubble. AI Assistance only explains how Trussen works and is on every plan. Trussen AI does work for you.

## Ask Trussen

Click **Ask Trussen** in the top bar to open the side panel. Ask in plain words, for example:

- "Create a bug for the login crash and assign it to Sara"
- "What's blocking the mobile project?"
- "Summarize this cycle"

Type **@** to mention members, projects, teams, departments or issues.

- **New chat** starts over. **History** shows past chats.
- **Stop** ends a reply. Anything already done is kept.

## Changes it makes

Before risky actions, it asks you to **Confirm** or **Cancel**. Every change it makes is listed under **Changes made**:

- **Keep** accepts it.
- **Undo** restores the old values.
- **Reject** marks something it created as rejected. It can't delete new items.

## Generate an issue

On **Create Issue**, describe the work in the **Trussen AI** box and click **Generate**. It fills in the form. Check it and edit before you click **Create**.

## AI suggestions

While you write an issue, **AI suggestions** can point out similar or duplicate issues and suggest labels, priority and assignees. **Dismiss** the ones you don't want.

## What it can see

Only what **your role** allows. It can't see or change anything you couldn't.

## Limits

Every request counts toward the workspace's daily AI limit. See *AI Usage*.

## Other plans

On Free and Standard, Ask Trussen, Generate and AI suggestions are hidden. The owner can upgrade to Premium in **Billing**.
