**AI Assistance** is the help bubble in the corner. Ask it how to use Trussen:

- where something is
- how a feature works
- what your role allows
- which page you are on

It is available on **every plan**.

## What it knows

- **The page you are on.**
- **Your role and your workspace's plan.**
- **Owners and admins only:** member and storage usage against the plan's limits.
- **Everyone except guests:** which integrations are connected.
- **Your recent questions,** so you can ask follow-ups like "and how do I change it?".

## Where answers come from

AI Assistance answers from these help articles and from your workspace's own settings. Answers show **Learn more** links to the articles they used.

If it can't find a basis for an answer, it says it isn't sure instead of guessing, shows the closest articles and offers **Contact support**.

## Your chat history

Your chat with AI Assistance is kept for **24 hours**, so it follows you to other devices, then it is deleted. Use **Clear chat** in the bubble to delete it sooner. Each workspace has its own chat, and only you can see yours.

## Feedback and improving the help

Use the thumbs under an answer to say whether it helped. A thumbs down asks what was wrong.

To improve these articles, the Trussen team sees questions people ask, and any feedback, **without names or workspaces**. Emails, phone numbers and links are removed first. This is kept for **90 days**. People in your workspace, including admins, can't see your questions.

## What it can't do

It only explains things. It does not create or change anything. For work like planning or creating issues, use **Trussen AI** (Premium).
