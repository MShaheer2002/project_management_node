Trussen is where your company plans, tracks and ships work. Everything happens inside a **workspace**: your company's own space, with its own address (like `yourcompany.trussen.app`), people, settings and plan.

## The building blocks

- **Issues:** single pieces of work. There are three types: **Task** (something to do), **Bug** (something broken) and **Issue** (a feature or request).
- **Projects:** group issues toward a goal, with a lead, dates and a roadmap.
- **Teams:** the people who work together. Every workspace starts with one team.
- **Departments:** group teams and people across the company, like Engineering or Sales.
- **Cycles:** short time boxes (sprints) that you plan issues into.
- **Roadmap:** your projects over time, with milestones and dependencies.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left has everything:

- **Your work:** Dashboard, Inbox (your notifications) and My issues.
- **Browse:** All issues and All projects.
- **Your teams:** under each team, its Issues, Projects, Team page, Cycles and Roadmap.
- **Organization:** Departments, Team directory, Members, Activity, Analytics and Integrations. Owners and admins also see Templates, Billing, AI Usage and Settings.

The top bar has search (**⌘K**, or Ctrl+K on Windows), **+** to create an issue, the notification bell and your profile menu.

## Your first steps

1. **Invite your team** from **Members**. See *Members and invitations*.
2. **Create a project** from **All projects**. See *Projects*.
3. **Create issues** with **+** in the top bar. See *Create an issue*.
4. **Plan a cycle** for the next week or two. See *Cycles (sprints)*.

## Getting help

- **AI Assistance:** the **Help** button in the bottom corner answers questions about Trussen.
- **Help articles:** open **Help articles** from that bubble to read and search all of them.
