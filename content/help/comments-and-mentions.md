Discuss work on the issue itself. Everyone in the workspace, guests included, can comment on issues they can see.

## Comment

1. Write in **Leave a comment…** at the bottom of the issue.
2. Add images or videos with **Attach** if you like.
3. Send it.

Use **Reply** on a comment to answer it directly.

## Mention someone

- Type **@** and pick a person. They get a notification in their **Inbox** (and in Slack, if it's connected).
- You can only mention people who can see the issue.
- One comment can mention up to **{{comment.maxMentions}}** people, and there is an hourly limit on mention notifications to prevent spam.

## Edit or delete

- **Edit:** your own comments.
- **Delete:** your own comments. Owners and admins can delete any comment.
