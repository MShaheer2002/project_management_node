**Activity** shows recent changes across the workspace: issues created and updated, status changes, comments, projects, teams, cycles and more.

## Where to find activity

- **Workspace activity:** **Activity** in the sidebar.
- **Scoped activity:** projects, teams, departments and cycles each have their own **Activity** tab, which shows only their events.
- **One issue's history:** at the bottom of the issue page.

## Searching

Use the search box at the top of the activity list to find a change by text.

## Privacy

You only see activity for things you have access to. Changes in private projects, teams or departments you aren't part of don't appear.
