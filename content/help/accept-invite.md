You've been invited to a workspace. Here's how to join.

## Join

1. Open the link in your invitation email.
2. Check the details: the workspace, your **Role**, **Designation**, **Team** and **Department**, and the email it was sent to.
3. Click **Accept**. If you don't have an account yet, sign up first; if you do, choose **Log in**. The invitation is kept while you do.

You'll land in the workspace.

## If it doesn't work

- **"Sent to another email":** sign in with the exact address the invite was sent to. If you signed up with a different email, sign out and use the right one.
- **"Expired":** invites last {{invite.expiryDays}} days. Ask an admin to send a new one.
- **"Cancelled" or "no longer valid":** an admin revoked it. Ask for a new one.
- **"Already accepted" or "already a member":** you're in already. Just sign in and open the workspace.
