You can belong to several workspaces, for example one per company or client. Each is completely separate.

## How workspaces are separated

- Each workspace has its **own address**, like `acme.trussen.app`.
- Each has its own **members, roles, projects, settings, plan and billing**. Your role can be different in each one.
- Nothing is shared between workspaces.

## Switch workspace

1. Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Pick another workspace from the list.

Trussen opens that workspace at its own address. Drafts from the workspace you left are cleared from this browser.

## Links always open the right workspace

Links in emails, Slack and Discord go straight to the workspace they belong to, so you won't land in the wrong one.

## Create another workspace

Choose to create a new workspace from the workspace menu. See *Create a workspace*.
