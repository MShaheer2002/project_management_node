A **department** groups teams and people across the company, like Engineering, Sales or Operations. Each department can have a **head**.

## Create a department

Only **owners and admins** can create departments.

1. Open **Departments** and click **Create Department**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Department name** and **Description**.
   - **Department head:** optional. You can create it without one.
   - **Visibility:** Public or Private.
   - **Theme color**, so it's easy to recognize.
   - **Members.**
   - **Make this the default department:** marks it with a **Default** badge in the list. Only one department can be the default.
3. Create it.

## The department page

- **Overview:** members, teams and projects at a glance.
- **Members**, **Teams** and **Projects** tabs.
- **Activity:** events for this department only.
- **Settings:** name, description, head and visibility.

## Manage a department

- **Edit settings and members:** the department head, admins and owners.
- **Delete:** owners and admins only, from **Danger Zone** in Settings.
- Workspace admins and owners can't be removed from a department. Change their role first.

## Public or private

- **Public:** everyone in the workspace can see the department.
- **Private:** only its head, its members, admins and owners can see it.

## Finding departments

Search the **Departments** list or filter by visibility. Each one shows its head, members, teams and projects.
