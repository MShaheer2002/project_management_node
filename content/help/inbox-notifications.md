The **Inbox** collects your notifications in one place.

## What you get notified about

- Someone **mentions** you in a comment.
- An issue is **assigned** to you.
- Changes to issues you **watch**.
- Due date reminders and **overdue** issues.
- **AI suggestions**, on Premium.

The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications are unread.

## Working through your inbox

- **Open:** click a notification to go to the issue or page it's about.
- **Mark one as read:** hover over it and click the check button.
- **Mark visible read:** marks everything currently shown (for example after searching) as read.
- **Mark all read:** clears everything.
- **Search:** use the search box to find an old notification.

## Getting notified elsewhere

Connect **Slack** to also get direct messages when you're assigned, mentioned or an issue is due soon. See *Slack*.
