Owners and admins open **Settings** from the sidebar. It has three tabs.

## General

- **Appearance:** Interface Theme: Light, Dark or System.
- **Workspace Profile:**
  - **Organization Name.**
  - **Workspace Logo:** PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, up to 5 MB.
  - **Workspace URL:** shown for reference. It can't be changed after the workspace is created.
- **Workspace docs:** shared files for everyone. See *Documents and folders*.
- **Danger Zone:** delete the workspace (owner only). See *Delete or restore a workspace*.

If you leave with unsaved changes, you're asked whether to save first.

## Workspace

- **Workflow:** statuses, transition rules and automations. See *Workflow statuses*.
- **Invite Access:** who can be invited (any domain, company only, or custom domains). Owner only.
- **File Uploads:**
  - **Upload Policy:** System Storage + Google Drive, System Storage Only, or Google Drive Only.
  - **Public Drive links:** whether files on personal Drives may be shared with anyone who has the link.

## Personal Access Tokens

API keys for tools and AI apps. See *AI Connections and API keys*.
