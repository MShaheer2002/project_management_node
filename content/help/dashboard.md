The **Dashboard** is your home page. It shows what is happening in your workspace today.

## What you see

- **Summary numbers:** issues completed, active projects, team members and open issues.
- **Velocity Overview:** how much work your teams finished over recent periods.
- **Sprint Progress:** how the current cycles are going.
- **Assigned to me:** your open work. **View all** opens *My issues*.
- **Team Activity:** recent changes across the workspace. Issue ids in the list (like `ACME-12`) are links that open the issue.
- **Active Projects** and **Upcoming Deadlines**.

## Good to know

- Use **New Issue** at the top to create an issue straight from the Dashboard.
- You only see work you have access to. Private projects, teams and departments you aren't part of are left out.
- Open it any time from **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar.
