A **project** groups issues toward a goal, like "Mobile app redesign". Each project belongs to a team and has a **lead**.

## Create a project

Members, admins and owners can create projects.

1. Open **All projects** and click **New Project**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Project Name** and **Description**.
   - **Team:** the team that owns it. Required.
   - **Department:** optional.
   - **Project Lead:** who's responsible. Required.
   - **Visibility:** **Public to workspace** or **Private to members**.
   - **Start Date** and **Target Date**, used by the roadmap.
   - **Members:** who works on it.
   - **Features:** turn **Roadmap**, **Cycles** and **Issue Tracking** on or off.
   - Owners and admins can also attach reference documents.
3. Click **Create Project**.

## Inside a project

- **Overview:** description, issue counts, progress, team and docs.
- **Issues** and **Board:** the project's work.
- **Roadmap:** dates, milestones and dependencies. See *Roadmap*.
- **Docs:** files and folders. See *Documents and folders*.
- **Members:** who's on the project.
- **Activity:** what changed.
- **Settings:** for the lead, admins and owners.

## Manage members

In the **Members** tab, use **Add Members** to add people, and **Remove** to take someone off. To remove the lead, first make someone else the lead.

## Settings

The project lead, admins and owners can change:

- name, description, lead, visibility, status and dates
- **Project Features**
- the project's **Workflow** (see *Project workflows and statuses*)

## Archive or delete

In **Settings**, under **Danger Zone**:

- **Archive:** marks the project inactive without deleting anything. You're asked to confirm. The lead, admins and owners can archive.
- **Delete Permanently:** removes the project and its linked data. **Owners and admins only.**

## Public or private

- **Public:** everyone in the workspace can see it.
- **Private:** only its members, its lead, admins and owners can see it.

## Finding projects

In **All projects**, filter by team, status (Active, Completed, Archived) and visibility, or search by name. Each project shows its lead, status and progress.
