Create a new workspace for your company. You become its **owner**.

## Steps

1. After signing up, or from the workspace menu, choose to create a workspace.
2. Enter your **Organization name**.
3. Pick your **Workspace URL**, like `acme` for `acme.trussen.app`. Trussen checks that it's available as you type.
4. Choose **How large is your team?** (1-5, 6-20, 21-50 or 50+).
5. Choose who can be invited:
   - **Anyone**, with any email address.
   - **Company only**, people with your email domain.
   - **Custom**, a list of domains you enter.
6. Create the workspace.

## What you get

- You are the **owner**, with full control, including billing.
- The workspace starts on the **Free** plan. See *Plans and limits*.
- A first **team** is created for you.

Next: invite your team (see *Members and invitations*) and create a project (see *Projects*).
