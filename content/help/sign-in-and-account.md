How to sign up, sign in, verify your email and reset your password.

## Sign up

1. Open the sign up page and enter your **full name**, **email** and a **password**, or choose **Google** or **GitHub**.
2. Enter the code we email you to verify your address.
3. Create your workspace, or accept an invitation to join one. See *Create a workspace* and *Accept an invitation*.

## Sign in

Sign in with your email and password, or with Google or GitHub. If you belong to more than one workspace, you'll pick which one to open.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign in page, click **Forgot?**.
2. Enter your email. We send you a reset code.
3. Enter the **Reset code**, choose a new password, confirm it, and save.

## Sign out

Use the menu under your name, or the sign out button at the bottom of the sidebar. When you sign out, drafts and other workspace data saved in this browser are cleared, so the next person on a shared computer can't see them.

## Trouble signing in

- **No code arrived:** check your spam folder, then use the resend option on the code screen.
- **"No access to this workspace":** you aren't a member of that workspace. Ask an admin to invite you, or switch to a workspace you belong to.
