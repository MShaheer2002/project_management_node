Add screenshots, recordings and files to issues and comments.

## On issues and comments

- **Images and videos** are stored in Trussen: drag them onto **Drop files here or click to browse**, or use **Add files**.
- **Any other file**, like a PDF or spreadsheet, goes through Google Drive with **Upload to Drive**. You need to connect your Drive first. See *Google Drive*.

| Type | Formats | Max size |
|---|---|---|
| Images | PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP | {{upload.imageMb}} MB |
| Videos | MP4, MOV, WebM | {{upload.videoMb}} MB |
| Any file through Google Drive | most files | {{drive.uploadMb}} MB |

## Documents

For PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, Markdown and text files that belong to a project, team or the whole workspace, use **Docs** instead (up to {{upload.documentMb}} MB each). See *Documents and folders*.

## Where files are stored

Your admins choose this in **Settings** under **Upload Policy**:

- **System Storage + Google Drive:** people can use either.
- **System Storage Only:** Trussen storage.
- **Google Drive Only:** Google Drive.

Files stored in Trussen are only shown to people in your workspace. Drive files follow the Drive's sharing setting.

## Who can upload

Members, admins and owners.

## Common messages

- **"Only images and videos can be attached":** use **Upload to Drive** for other files.
- **"Connect Google Drive to upload files":** connect your Drive in **Integrations** first.
