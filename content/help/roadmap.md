The **Roadmap** shows your projects on a timeline, with their milestones and how they depend on each other.

## The roadmap page

Open **Roadmap** in the sidebar (or a team's Roadmap).

- **Filter** by team, department, status and health, or search for a project.
- **Unscheduled** lists projects without dates. Add a start and target date to put them on the timeline.
- Projects that are **Blocked** by another project are marked.
- **Outside this timeline** shows projects whose dates fall outside the visible range.

Members, admins and owners can open the roadmap page. Guests can only see an individual project's roadmap.

## A project's roadmap

Open a project and go to its **Roadmap** tab. It shows start and target dates, progress, milestones, health, a forecast, and whether it's blocked.

### Schedule

Set the **Start date** and **Target date**. You can add a reason for the change, which is useful when dates slip.

### Milestones

Key checkpoints inside the project.

- **Add:** enter a **Name** and **Due date** (both required), plus an optional description.
- **Reorder:** drag milestones into order.
- **Edit or delete** a milestone.
- A milestone dated outside the project's dates is marked **Out of range**.

### Dependencies between projects

- **Upstream:** projects this one depends on. Use **Select blocking project** to add one.
- **Downstream:** projects waiting on this one.
- When the blocking work is done, **Resolve** the dependency. You can also **Cancel** or **Delete** it.
- Trussen stops you from creating a loop (A waits on B, B waits on A).

## Who can change the roadmap

People who manage the project (its lead, admins and owners) can change dates, milestones and dependencies.
