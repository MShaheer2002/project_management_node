Break work into steps with **subtasks**, and connect related work with **dependencies**.

## Subtasks

Subtasks are small steps inside an issue, like a checklist.

- **Add:** type in **Add a subtask…** and press **Enter**.
- **Complete:** tick the box. When every subtask is done, Trussen suggests marking the issue Done. Your admins can also set the workflow to move it automatically. See *Workflow statuses*.
- **Reorder:** drag a subtask up or down.
- **Edit or delete:** use the subtask's controls.

Templates can add subtasks automatically from their checklist. See *Issue templates*.

## Dependencies

Dependencies show how issues affect each other. On the issue page, under **Relations**, or in **System Parameters** when creating an issue:

- **Blocks:** this issue must be done before the other one can start.
- **Blocked by:** this issue waits on the other one.
- **Related:** they're connected, with no order.

To change a relation's type, pick the new type. If the change can't be saved, the old link is put back.

## Parent issues

To put an issue under a bigger one, set its **Parent Issue**.

## Who can do this

Members, admins and owners.
