**My issues** collects your own work. Open it from **My issues** in the sidebar.

## Tabs

- **Assigned to Me:** issues assigned to you.
- **Created by Me:** issues you created.
- **Completed:** issues assigned to you that reached a finished (Done) status, in any project's workflow.

## Views and filters

- Switch between **List** and **Board**.
- Search by title or id, and filter by type and project.
- Select issues for bulk actions: change status, **Add To Cycle**, or **Remove** (owners and admins only).

**Assigned to me** on the Dashboard shows the same work at a glance.
