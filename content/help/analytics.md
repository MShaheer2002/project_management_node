**Analytics** shows how work is going. Open **Analytics** in the sidebar.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Workspace-wide numbers | Owners and admins |
| Project | One project's progress | Members, admins, owners |
| Team | One team's progress and workload | Team leads, admins, owners |
| Member | One person's work | Admins and owners can pick anyone; members see their own |
| Cycle | One cycle's progress | Members, admins, owners |

Pick the project, team, member or cycle at the top of the tab.

## Period

Choose **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.

## Export

Download the data as CSV with the export button. Team and cycle exports are for owners and admins.

## Velocity and progress

The **Dashboard** also shows **Velocity Overview** and **Sprint Progress** at a glance. See *Dashboard*.
