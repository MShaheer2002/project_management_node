A **cycle** is a short, fixed period of work for one team, like a two-week sprint. You plan issues into it, work through them, then close it.

## Create a cycle

Members, admins and owners can create cycles.

1. Open **Cycles** (in the sidebar, or under a team) and click **New Cycle**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**, like "Cycle 16".
   - **Goal:** what the cycle should deliver. Required.
   - **Start Date** and **End Date**. The end must be after the start.
   - **Team Scope:** the team. Required.
   - **Carry over unfinished issues on completion**, and **Notify team members when cycle starts**.
3. Click **Create Cycle**. It starts as **Upcoming**.

## Plan work

Open the cycle and click **Plan Issues** to add existing issues from the team, or **Add New Issue** to create one directly in the cycle. To take an issue out, select it and choose **Remove From Cycle**. You can also add issues from the issues list with **Add To Cycle**.

## Track progress

The cycle page shows:

- **Goal**, **Days left** and **Time elapsed**.
- **Scope completion** and a **Scope breakdown** by status.
- The issues as a **List**, **Board** or **Calendar** (the calendar shows the cycle's real dates), with search and an assignee filter.

## How completion is calculated

A cycle's progress, or completion percentage, is the share of its issues that are finished, rounded to a whole percent. For example, 18 finished out of 24 issues is 75%. An issue counts as finished when it is in a Done status.

## The cycle's life

A cycle moves **Upcoming → Current → Completed**. Open **Open Control Center** to:

- **Start Cycle** and **Complete Cycle**.
- **Carry Over** unfinished issues to another cycle.
- Change the **Start date** and **End date**, then **Save dates**.
- **Reopen Cycle:** owners and admins only.
- **Delete Cycle**, in the Danger zone.

## Who can do what

Members, admins and owners can create, plan, start, complete, carry over and delete cycles. Reopening is for owners and admins. Guests can view cycles.
