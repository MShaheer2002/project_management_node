A **team** is a group of people who work together, like "Platform" or "Mobile". Each team has a **lead**, and its own issues, projects, cycles and roadmap in the sidebar under **Your teams**.

## Create a team

Members, admins and owners can create teams.

1. Open **Team directory** and click **Create Team**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Team name** and **Description**.
   - **Team lead**.
   - **Department:** optional.
   - **Members.**
   - Owners and admins can also attach starting documents.
3. Create it.

On the Free plan a workspace can have **{{free.teamCap}} teams**, including the one it starts with. Standard and Premium have no limit.

## The team page

- **Members:** who's on the team, with their role and department. Use **Invite** to add workspace members to the team.
- **Projects** the team owns, with their progress.
- **Docs:** team files. See *Documents and folders*.
- **Activity:** the team's recent changes.
- **Member Performance:** completion progress per person. Visible to the team lead, admins and owners.
- **Settings:** name, description, lead and visibility.

## Manage a team

- **Edit settings and members:** the team lead, admins and owners.
- **Remove a member:** use **Remove member**. To remove the lead, first make someone else the lead.
- **Delete the team:** owners and admins only.

## Public or private

- **Public:** everyone in the workspace can see the team.
- **Private:** only its members, its lead, admins and owners can see it.

## Finding teams

Search **Team directory** by name, or filter by visibility.
