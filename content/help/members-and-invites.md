Manage who is in your workspace from **Members** in the sidebar.

## Invite someone

Owners and admins can invite people.

1. Open **Members** and click **Invite Member**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Email Address.**
   - **Role:** Admin, Member or Guest. See *Roles and permissions*.
   - **Designation:** their job title.
   - **Team Assignment:** the team they'll join.
   - **Department:** optional.
3. Send the invite.

They get an email with a link that's valid for **{{invite.expiryDays}} days**. They must sign in with **the same email** the invite was sent to. See *Accept an invitation*.

## Who can be invited

The owner decides this in **Settings**, under **Invite Access**:

- **Any domain:** anyone.
- **Company only:** only people with your company's email domain.
- **Custom:** only the domains you list.

A workspace can send up to 50 invites an hour.

## Pending invitations

Open invites are listed under **Pending Invitations**. Use **Revoke** to cancel one. You're asked to confirm, and the link stops working.

## Change someone's role

Owners and admins can change a member's role (Admin, Member or Guest) from the role menu in their row. A workspace has exactly one owner: the owner can't be demoted, and nobody can be made owner.

## Remove someone

Owners and admins can **Remove** a member, after confirming. They lose access to the workspace at once. Their past work, like issues and comments, stays. The owner can't be removed.

## Finding people

Search by name or email, and filter by role or department.

## Free plan limit

On Free, members plus pending invites together can't go over **{{free.memberCap}}**. Revoke unused invites to make room, or upgrade. See *Plans and limits*.
