Open any issue to see everything about it and change it.

## Editing

Click any field to change it:

- **Title** and **Description**.
- For bugs: **Steps to Reproduce**, **Expected Behavior** and **Actual Behavior**. For issues: **Acceptance Criteria**.
- **Status**, **Priority**, **Assignee**, **Due Date** and **Due Time**.
- **Labels**, **Cycle**, and the project, team and department it belongs to.

Changes save as you make them.

## Also on the issue page

- **Subtasks** and **Relations** (dependencies). See *Subtasks and dependencies*.
- **Attachments:** add files with **Add files**. See *Attachments and file uploads*.
- **Comments:** discuss the work and mention teammates. See *Comments and mentions*.
- **Watchers**, **Parent Issue** and **Integration References**.
- **Design previews** from Figma and **GitHub activity**, when those integrations are connected.
- **Activity:** the full history of changes.

## Share an issue

Use **Copy issue link** or **Share issue** at the top. The link opens this issue in your workspace.

## Delete an issue

**Delete issue** is at the top. Only **owners and admins** can delete issues, and you're asked to confirm.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Guest | View and comment |
| Member | Edit everything above, add subtasks, attachments and links |
| Admin, Owner | All of the above, plus delete |
